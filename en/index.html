helpWhen is handover?

Blocks A and B are planned for September 2027, C and D for March 2028 (fictional schedule).

helpCan I see the show flat?

There is a furnished 3-bed show flat above the sales office, open daily 10:00 – 19:00 without an appointment.

helpWhat is the payment plan?

Down payment and instalments vary by flat; a personal plan is prepared in writing at the sales office. Prices and availability are fictional; the current list is given in writing at the sales office.

helpIs there parking?

Each flat gets one covered parking space; visitor parking is at the entrance and the garden is car-free.

helpHow do I get to the metro?

About a 6-minute walk to Gorukle station; a bus line towards the university passes the entrance.

helpAre pets allowed?

Yes; the garden has a leashed walking path and a separate dog area.
